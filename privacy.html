Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Vandelay Industries of SWFL, LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile apps and software we publish (the "Apps"). Individual Apps may have additional details listed on their store pages.

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing us at the address below.
