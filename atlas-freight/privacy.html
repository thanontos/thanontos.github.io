Atlas Freight

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 11, 2026

In short: Atlas Freight does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

Atlas Freight (the “game”) is a rail and harbor puzzle game published on Google Play by Vandelay Interactive (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other personal information, and it does not send data over the internet.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a small amount of data in its own private storage on your device:

  • your current game in progress (the map, rail lines, trains, and score);
  • your best scores; and
  • your settings, such as music and sound-effect volume and the casual crossings option.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes it permanently.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries, and we do not sell or share information with anyone. Google Play, which you use to download the game, has its own privacy policy that covers your Google account and the Play Store.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
